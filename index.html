Application: HAZIR (Human and AI Zone for IT Resolution)
Effective date: October 13, 2026
Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

HAZIR ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is an internal task tracking application operated by [Company Name] ("the Company"). It lets employees in different departments submit requests to the IT Team and follow the progress of those requests until they are resolved.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, who can see it, and the choices you have. By using HAZIR you agree to the practices described here.

2. Who This Policy Applies To

HAZIR is intended only for authorized employees and personnel of the Company. This includes:

  • Requesters (RI): staff from other departments who raise requests to the IT Team.
  • Supervisors (SUP): staff who monitor requests for their departments or applications.
  • IT Team members (DEV): staff who pick up, work on and resolve requests.
  • Heads of Department / Line Managers (LM): staff who oversee workload and progress.

The App is not intended for the general public and is not directed at children.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Account and profile information

When you sign in, the App receives information from the Company's systems that identifies you and your role, such as:

  • Your user ID and full name
  • Your department
  • Your role or category within the App (for example Requester, Supervisor, IT Team member or Head of Department)

3.2 Request and task information

When requests are created or updated, the App stores the details of that request, including:

  • Title and description of the request
  • Application or project the request relates to
  • Category (for example Support or Development)
  • How the request was received (for example email, phone or chat)
  • The name of the person who requested it and the IT Team member who picked it
  • Dates and times: requested on, estimated completion, extended completion and completed date
  • Status (Requested, In Process, On Hold, Completed, Cancelled) and status remarks
  • Reasons entered for putting a task on hold or extending its completion date
  • Completion remarks entered when a task is closed

3.3 Files and attachments

If you attach files to a request, the App accesses only the files you choose to select on your device. We do not scan or access other files on your device.

To keep the App working, our servers and your device may process basic technical data such as app version, device type and operating system, and error or diagnostic information. [Describe here any analytics or crash-reporting tools you use, or delete this line if none are used.]

3.5 Information we do not collect

HAZIR does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos (unless you choose to attach one), microphone or camera data, or payment information.

We use the information above only for legitimate business purposes, namely to:

  • Let you sign in and give you access appropriate to your role
  • Create, assign, route and track requests to the IT Team
  • Show request status, deadlines and history to the people involved
  • Provide dashboards and summaries so supervisors and managers can monitor workload and progress
  • Send or display notifications about request updates [if applicable]
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot and improve the App
  • Meet the Company's internal policy, audit and legal obligations

5. Who Can See Your Information

Access inside the App is based on role:

  • Requesters can see the requests they raised and their progress.
  • Supervisors can see requests for the applications and requesters they are responsible for.
  • IT Team members and Heads of Department can also see which team member picked a task and the team members assigned to it.
  • IT Team members can update the status of tasks they handle.

Administrators of the Company's IT systems may access App data as needed to operate and support it.

6. Sharing of Information

We share information only in these cases:

  • Within the Company, with the people and departments involved in handling a request.
  • Service providers who host, maintain or support the App and its servers, and who are bound to protect the data.
  • Legal or regulatory reasons, when required by law, court order or a valid request from authorities.
  • Business changes, such as a restructuring, where data may be transferred to a successor under the same protections.

We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing.

7. Data Storage and Security

App data is transmitted to and stored on servers managed by or on behalf of the Company. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it, including role-based access, authenticated sign-in and secure connections. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your login credentials confidential and log out when using a shared device.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law and Company policy, you may ask to:

  • Access the personal information the App holds about you
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or out of date
  • Request deletion of your information where it is no longer needed

To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 12. Some records, such as completed task history, may need to be kept for business or legal reasons.
